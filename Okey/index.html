About Okey

Okey Offline: Turkish Rummy brings the classic Turkish game of Okey - also known as Turkish Rummy - to your phone and tablet. You can play offline too, with no internet, no account is needed and it's free - just you, the tiles and the rack. Play 4-player Okey against three computer opponents; no waiting, always ready.

Play Okey vs Computer

Challenge the computer on three levels: Easy, Medium and Hard. Win 3 matches on Easy to unlock Medium, and 5 matches on Medium to unlock Hard. Choose a match goal of 10, 20 or 30 points. Show the indicator, finish with the okey, go for pairs.

Okey Tutorial: How to Play Okey

Never played before? 9 interactive lessons teach you step by step: the tiles and the table, the indicator and the okey, building sets, your turn (draw and discard), taking the discard, finishing the hand, using the okey, going for pairs, the indicator and scoring. You drag and arrange the tiles yourself; a hint appears when you need one.

Hints: The Best Move

Stuck? The Hint button shows the draw or discard chosen by the strongest computer level right on the table. When you run out of hint tokens, you get a free one in your first new match of the day - and you can also earn a token by watching a short ad.

Your Rules

Allow taking a discarded okey or not; when the deck runs out, end the hand in a draw or reshuffle the discards and play on; let the winner or the next player start the new hand. Set the game to the way you like to play Okey.

Easy Tile Sorting

Drag and drop your tiles on a two-row rack. Sort by runs or by groups with one tap. Double-tap a tile to discard it. If Auto play is on, the game plays your turn when your time runs out.

Your Table

4 table themes, game speed setting, your profile name and 30 avatars.

Always Ready

Your unfinished match is saved automatically; continue where you left off. Follow your progress in Statistics.

Google Play Games

Unlock 10 achievements and take your place on the Okey Points and Hard level leaderboards. Google Play Games is optional; no account is needed to play.

Designed for phone and tablet; free and playable offline. Internet is needed for Play Games achievements and leaderboards. Available in Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese.

Okey is a classic tile game of luck and strategy, loved in Turkey and known around the world as Turkish Rummy: 106 tiles, an indicator and the okey wild tile.

Sort your tiles, finish the hand, play Okey offline - anytime, anywhere.
