Privacy Policy

Tavla

Backgammon Offline: Classic (the "App") is a free, ad-supported backgammon game played against the computer or by two people on the same device, developed by Nurettin BARDAKCI. This policy explains what information is kept where when you use the App, which information is processed by third parties (Google), and what choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy. Terms used here have the same meaning as in the Terms & Conditions inside the App.

In short

  • You do not need an account; I have no server and your game data is not sent to me.
  • Your player name, avatar, statistics and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Some data is processed by Google because of ads (Google AdMob) and the optional Google Play Games services; details are below.

Data Controller

Because I have no server of my own, your game data never reaches me. Ad data and Google Play Games data are processed by Google under its own privacy policy and on its own responsibility. For this policy and any questions about your data, contact: nbardakci1975@gmail.com

Data Stored on Your Device

Your player name, avatar, game settings, statistics, unfinished match, tutorial progress and hint tokens are stored only on your device. They are not sent to me or to any other server. They are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear the App's storage in Android settings.

Google Play Games Services

When the App starts, it tries to sign you in to Google Play Games Services automatically; if you do not sign in or you decline, the game can still be played with all its features (except achievements and leaderboards) without signing in, and you can sign in later using the Play Games button in the App. If you sign in, the App receives your Play Games player name and profile picture from Google and keeps them on your device to show on your player profile; you can change your name and avatar in the App at any time. Your achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Google Play Games Services and are processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can sign out, and manage or delete your Play Games data in Google Play Games settings and your Google Account.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner, interstitial (full-screen) and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Watching a rewarded ad is optional; if you watch it, you earn hint tokens. To serve ads, measure their performance, help prevent fraud and (if you have given consent) personalize ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects the following data from your device and shares it with Google:

  • IP address; it may be used to estimate the approximate (general) location of your device
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, ad impressions, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, crash and hang information and energy usage

This data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and advertising policies. It does not reach me; I only see aggregated ad performance reports. Ads are requested with a content rating suitable for an adult audience. For this purpose the App uses Android's advertising ID permission (AD_ID) and the internet access permission.

Ad Consent and Ad Personalization

In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form is shown through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) when the App is first opened. In this form you choose whether to consent to the processing of your data for personalized ads; declining does not prevent you from using the App, you will simply see non-personalized ads. Where consent is required, you can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the GDPR button at the bottom left of the main screen. In other regions you can turn off ad personalization in your Android device settings (Google > Ads), and reset or delete your advertising ID.

In-App Updates and In-App Review

The App uses Google Play In-App Updates to tell you when a new version is available, and Google Play In-App Review so that you can optionally rate the App. These operations are carried out by the Google Play Store. The rating and review you give are sent directly to Google Play and processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy; they are not sent to me.

Third-Party Services Used

  • Google Play Services (Google LLC)
  • Google Play Games Services (Google LLC)
  • Google Play In-App Updates and In-App Review (Google LLC)
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (Google LLC)
  • Unity (Unity Technologies)

The App is built with the Unity game engine; the engine runs on your device. Unity's analytics, advertising and crash-reporting services are not used.

You can read the privacy policies of these services on their own websites (for example policies.google.com/privacy). These third parties process data only to provide their own services.

Diagnostics and Log Data

I have no error-reporting or analytics service of my own; I do not collect logs about how you use the App. The diagnostic information mentioned above is sent to Google only by Google's SDKs.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App does not use cookies. Google's advertising SDK may use the advertising ID and similar identifiers for the purposes described above.

Transfer of Data Abroad

Google may process data on servers in different countries around the world. Transfers abroad take place through the Google SDKs that collect this data and in line with Google's transfer mechanisms (for example standard contractual clauses); optional processing such as ad personalization is carried out only if you give consent.

Retention and Deleting Your Data

The App has no account system. You can delete the data on your device at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the App. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. How long data processed by Google is kept is governed by Google's policies; you can manage or delete Play Games data in your Google Account.

Security

Android's app isolation is used to protect the data on your device. Data sent to Google is transmitted over an encrypted connection. Still, please remember that no method of transmission or storage is one hundred percent secure; I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at anyone under the age of 18, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If I learn that a child under 18 has provided personal information, I will delete it where possible or notify Google. If you are a parent or guardian and notice that your child is using the App, please contact me.

Your Rights

Under Article 11 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), you have the right to: learn whether your personal data is processed; request information if it has been processed; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties to whom it is transferred; request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate; request deletion or destruction under the conditions set by law; request that these actions be notified to third parties to whom the data has been transferred; object to a result against you arising from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you also have (under the GDPR) the rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict the processing of and object to the processing of your data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw any consent you have given at any time; you may lodge a complaint with your competent data protection authority.

Because most data is on your device or with Google, you can exercise these rights through your device or Google Account settings; for matters that may concern me, you can contact me at the e-mail address below. Your request will be answered within thirty days at the latest.

Links to Other Sites

The App may link to third-party pages, such as Google's privacy form. I am not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those pages; I recommend that you read the relevant policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this policy from time to time; the current version is published on this page and inside the App. For important changes, I will try to inform you inside the App.
